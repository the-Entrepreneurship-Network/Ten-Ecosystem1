# Fit and tracker

## Fit 1–5 (resume only)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Title match + most must-have skills evidenced |
| 4 | Title adjacent + core stack evidenced |
| 3 | Partial stack, honest junior/intern stretch |
| 2 | Weak overlap |
| 1 | Wrong function — do not list unless they asked for stretch |

Must-haves with zero evidence → skip or mark Not claimed. Do not list a role that needs 3 years if the ledger is campus-only, unless it is explicitly an intern posting.

## Tracker row

Append to `job-tracker.md` when they say save, email, or tailor:

```
| date | company | role | url | fit | status | notes |
```

status: found | emailed | tailored | applied | closed

Do not create duplicate URLs.
