# Recruiter scan rules

Most humans do not read a resume. They scan. Write for three passes.

## Pass 1 — 6 seconds

They look at:

1. Name and current/most recent title
2. Summary first sentence
3. Skills line
4. Most recent employer + dates

If those four do not say "this is the job I am hiring," they bounce.

Top-third checklist (must all be true):

- Target role noun is visible without scrolling
- 5–12 JD-aligned skills are visible
- Latest job looks like the same function
- One proof spike is visible (scale, outcome, or domain)

## Pass 2 — 30 seconds

They skim the latest 2 roles and maybe projects.

Each recent bullet must answer one of:

- What did you build or own?
- With what tools?
- At what scale or with what result?

If a bullet cannot answer at least two of those, rewrite or delete it.

## Pass 3 — interview screen

They look for contradictions: skills not in the work, title inflation, impossible metrics, gaps.

Every skill in the Skills section needs a home in Experience or Projects. If it cannot live there, remove it.

## What HR actually screens for (controllable)

- Function match (engineer vs analyst vs marketer)
- Seniority match (not intern language for a senior req, not staff language for a junior)
- Tooling match
- Communication (clean, specific, short)
- Risk (instability, vagueness, sloppiness)

You cannot control headcount, visa, internal referrals, or whether the req is real.

## Density

- Summary: 3–4 lines max
- Skills: 1–3 lines, no paragraphs
- Bullet: one line preferred, two max
- Whitespace between sections, not inside a bullet
- No objective, no references-on-request, no photo, no soft-skill cloud ("team player, go-getter")

## Soft skills

Do not list them. Demonstrate them inside a bullet:

- Leadership → `Led 4 engineers to ship …`
- Communication → `Wrote the API spec adopted by 3 squads …`
- Problem solving → `Debugged … which restored …`

## Inclusive / safe wording

- No age, photo, marital status, religion, full street address
- City, region or Remote is enough
- Gendered or macho clichés ("ninja", "rockstar") out

## The bounce test (run on every draft)

Read only:

- Contact + summary + skills + first job header + first 2 bullets

If a stranger cannot say the target job and one reason to interview, the draft fails the HR-scan gate. Rewrite the top third before touching older roles.
