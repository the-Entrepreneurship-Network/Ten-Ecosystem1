Common questions

The things people ask us most.

Is the internship free?

45 Days, 3 Months and 6 Months are free. The shorter crash tracks — 1 Week, 15 Days and 1 Month — carry a one-time fee, because they compress the same material into a much shorter period with more coordinator time per intern.

What attendance do I need?

75%. Sundays are not counted, so they cannot hurt your percentage. You can see your current figure and your target any day inside the portal.

Is it remote?

Yes, entirely. Everything — tasks, submissions, attendance, coordinator reviews and chat — happens in the portal, so you can intern from anywhere.

What do I receive at the end?

A Letter of Completion and, if you meet the criteria, a Letter of Recommendation. Outstanding contributors can also earn Star Performer recognition. Every document carries a number anyone can check on this page.

Can I change my domain or internship length later?

Your domain and length are set when you register. If something was entered wrongly, ask your coordinator — they can correct it.
