01 · LEARN

The Academic Portal

One module for your domain — 21 topics that start gentle and climb to hard. Every topic is written twice: the technical explanation for depth, then the simple one so it actually lands. After each topic comes a video, then a proctored exam — ten written answers marked by AI with feedback on every one, ten multiple choice. Pass, and the next topic opens. At the end: a two-hour final, an optional big project, and a certificate anyone can verify online.
How it helps you as an intern: your internship tasks stop being guesswork — the module teaches exactly the domain your tasks come from, and the certificate is proof that survives any interview's "so what do you actually know?"
🧑‍💻
02 · INTERN

A real internship

A coordinator, weekly tasks, attendance that counts, and work that is reviewed — not busywork. Your task journey runs through the same portal, week by week, with coins for everything you finish and a record every reviewer can see.
How it helps you: "fresher, no experience" stops being true. You leave with reviewed work, a coordinator who can vouch for you, and documents — offer letter, completion, recommendation — generated from what you actually did.
🧭
03 · BE GUIDED

Mentorship, on call

One-to-one sessions with people who already do the job you are aiming at. Book a slot, bring the thing you are stuck on — a project decision, an interview coming up, a career fork — and leave with a plan. Domain coordinators also stay on call through hackathon nights.
How it helps you: the difference between six months of wandering and six weeks of aimed effort is usually one person who has already walked it. That is what a mentor session is for.
📄
04 · BE READY

The Resume Portal

Your resume rebuilt to pass the software that reads it before any human does — then checked line by line against the actual job description you want. Export it, reuse it, keep it current as your internship adds real work to it.
How it helps you: most resumes die in the ATS filter, unread. Yours goes through, carrying the reviewed projects and certificate the rest of the Studio just gave you.
💼
05 · GET HIRED

The Job Portal

An agent that hunts live openings across the web, matched to your domain and level, and applies on your behalf — every application tracked in one place instead of seventeen tabs and a spreadsheet.
How it helps you: the job hunt becomes a pipeline you watch, not a second job you do. You spend the hours preparing for interviews; the agent spends them applying.
⚡
06 · COMPETE

Hackathons & ideathons

48 hours, one repo, a demo that has to run. Enter solo and get matched into a team by stack and timezone, or bring your own. Judged on working code by a published rubric; every finisher leaves with a verifiable certificate and a public repo.
How it helps you: one weekend produces the strongest line on a fresher's resume — a shipped thing, with teammates and a deadline, that a recruiter can actually click.
