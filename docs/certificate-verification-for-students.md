# How to check a TEN certificate is real

For students, employers, and anyone who has been handed a TEN document.

No account needed. It takes about ten seconds.

---

## Why this exists

Anyone can make a PDF that says whatever they like. A TEN document is different:
every one we issue is recorded, and anybody can look it up on our website. That
means:

- **For you** — an employer can confirm your certificate is genuine straight
  away, without emailing us and waiting.
- **For an employer** — you can tell a real TEN certificate from one somebody
  edited or invented.

---

## Step 1 — Find the document number

Every TEN document has one, printed on the document itself. It looks like this:

```
TEN-LOC-2026-A3F91C
```

It is usually near the top or in the footer, often labelled **Document No.**,
**Reference**, or **Ref**.

Depending on the document, it starts with:

| Starts with | Document |
|---|---|
| `TEN-OL` | Offer Letter |
| `TEN-LOC` | Letter of Completion |
| `TEN-LOR` | Letter of Recommendation |
| `TEN-EXP`, `TEN-ND`, `TEN-FEL` | Expert / Nano Degree / Fellowship certificate |

Students: your documents are in **My Documents** and **My Certificates** in your
portal, and the number is on each one.

---

## Step 2 — Go to the verification page

Open:

**https://virtualinternships.entrepreneurshipnetwork.net/verify**

You do not need to log in. You do not need a TEN account. It works from any
phone or computer.

Some certificates also carry a QR code — scanning it opens this page with the
number already filled in.

---

## Step 3 — Type the number and check

Enter the document number exactly as printed and press **Verify**.

Capital letters and small letters do not matter. Do include the dashes.

---

## What you will see

### If the document is genuine

A confirmation showing:

- The student's name
- Their TEN Employee ID
- What the document is (Offer Letter, Letter of Completion, and so on)
- Their domain — for example Web Development
- Their college
- The date it was issued
- Issued by: The Entrepreneurship Network (TEN)

If those details match the document in your hand, it is real.

### If the document is not genuine

**"Document not found."**

That means this number was never issued by TEN. Usually it is a typo — check
for a `0` typed as an `O`, or a missing dash, and try again.

If the number is definitely right and it still says not found, the document was
not issued by us. Please tell us at **hr@entrepreneurshipnetwork.net**.

---

## What we do *not* show

The verification page shows only what is needed to confirm the document. It
never shows:

- Any password or login details
- The student's email address or phone number
- Their attendance, marks, or performance scores
- Any payment or fee information
- Any other document belonging to them

So it is safe to share a document number with an employer. It lets them confirm
the certificate — it does not give them access to anything, and it cannot be
used to log in as anyone.

---

## Common questions

**Someone has my document number. Is that a problem?**
No. It is designed to be shared — that is the point. It only confirms the
document exists and who it belongs to.

**I lost my certificate. Can I get the number back?**
Yes. Sign in and open **My Documents** or **My Certificates** — every document
you have been issued is listed there with its number.

**My certificate is not verifying.**
Check the number character by character first. If it is definitely correct,
email **hr@entrepreneurshipnetwork.net** with your name, Employee ID and the
document number.

**Can I verify someone else's certificate?**
Yes — that is exactly what it is for. If a candidate gives you a TEN document
number, you can check it yourself.

**Does the page ever go out of date?**
No. Once a document is issued it stays verifiable.
